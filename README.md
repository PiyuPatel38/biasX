# 🎭 BiasX: The Mirror of Ethics

> **"Technology doesn’t have bias — but those who build it can. Let’s change that."**

**BiasX** is an upcoming AI-powered Augmented Reality (AR) and interactive web platform that immerses users in real-world scenarios to reveal and challenge **gender bias**. Combining immersive storytelling, ethical decision scoring, emotion recognition, and 3D visuals, BiasX transforms digital interaction into ethical reflection.

---

## 🧠 Project Overview

BiasX introduces users to simulated social environments — classrooms, offices, digital spaces — where they engage in real-time dialogue, make choices, and see how their responses shape outcomes. An AI engine analyzes decisions to detect subtle gender bias, and a “Bias Mirror” visually reflects its social impact.

> The goal: Help users recognize and confront unconscious bias through interaction, not lecture.

---

## 🎯 Core Objectives

- 🌍 Visualize gender bias through immersive 3D interactions
- 🧠 Use NLP and AI to detect biased language and ethical behavior
- 🪞 Display user choices through a “Bias Mirror” — changing environments based on ethics
- 📊 Score and summarize decisions for self-reflection and awareness
- 🎓 Educate users with real-world stats and takeaways
